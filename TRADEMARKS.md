## Scope of the design

ORC does not implement, emulate, document, or interoperate with any Motorola proprietary protocol or interface, including the GCAI link used by the donor unit. The ORC controller board is an independent design that replaces the donor unit's controller board entirely. Control is plain CAN over standard terminal blocks.

No Motorola firmware, source code, schematic, drawing, photograph, or manual text is reproduced or redistributed in this repository. Where a fact about the donor unit is drawn from Motorola's published service or installation documentation, it is cited by section rather than reproduced.
